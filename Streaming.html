Crunchyroll

Crunchyroll, LLC is an American entertainment company based in Coppell, Texas. It is a streaming service offering a vast selection of Anime and movies from Japan and East Asia that includes both old and new titles. 

It is subscription based, offering 3 different plans varying in power and price, with cheapest plan starting $7.99 and going all the way up to $15.99 a month, including annual options for those interested. Each tier includes things like ad-free streaming, offline downloads and streaming on multiple devices at a time, with the highest tier offering streaming on 6 devices simultaneously, goodie bags and special discounts on the Crunchyroll store.

Soul eater aired on Crunchyroll on June 21st, 2022,and included several different languages both subbed and dubbed.

Prime Video

Prime video is Amazon's streaming service, offering a wide variety of shows and movies and live sports that can be watched through Amazon's Prime subscription or through a standalone subscription to Prime Video.

The service primarily distributes films and television series produced or co-produced by Amazon MGM Studios or licensed to Amazon, as Amazon Originals, with the service also hosting content from other providers, content add-ons, live sporting events, and video rental and purchasing services. Prime Video is offered both as a stand-alone service and as part of Amazon's Prime subscription. Amazon Prime Video is the second-most-subscribed video on demand streaming media service in the United States, after Netflix, with 205 million paid memberships.

Soul Eater is available on Prime Video in some regions, though you may need to purchase it alongside a full-fledged Amazon Prime or Prime Video Subscription.

Apple TV

Apple TV is a digital media player and a microconsole developed and marketed by Apple. It is a small piece of networking hardware that sends received media data such as video and audio to a TV or external display. Its media services include streaming media, TV Everywhere–based services, local media sources, sports journalism and broadcasts.

Second-generation and later models function only when connected via HDMI to an enhanced-definition or high-definition widescreen television. Since the fourth-generation model, Apple TV runs tvOS with multiple pre-installed apps. In November 2019, Apple released Apple TV+ and the Apple TV app.

The Apple TV app allows you to subscribe to various services, including Crunchyroll. Since the original Soul Eater anime is no longer available on Netflix, you may find it on Crunchyroll's platform.You can find and purchase or rent Soul Eater from other digital retailers within the Apple TV app. For example, searching for "Soul Eater" in the Apple TV app will show options to buy or rent the series from different providers.

Maka Albarn

Maka Albarn is the main protagonist of the manga/anime series Soul Eater. She is a student at the Death Weapon Meister Academy and a Scythe Meister. Her weapon partner is Soul Eater Evans and she wants to become a better meister just like her mother and strives to make Soul into a Death Scythe.

Soul Evans

Soul Eater Evans or just 'Soul' to his friends is the main male protagonist and the overall main protagonist of the manga/anime series Soul Eater and Maka Albarn's partner. He is also part of the group called Spartoi.

Franken Stein

Franken Stein is an eccentric but extremely talented doctor and Meister who is known to be the strongest Meister to have graduated from Death Weapon Meister Academy and formerly Spirit Albarn's first weapon-partner.

Spirit Albarn

Spirit Albarn is a major protagonist of the anime/manga series Soul Eater. He is a Demon Weapon, a human that has the ability to transform into a weapon to be wielded by a Meister. In Spirit's case, he can transform into a scythe. First wielded by Franken Stein, he becomes a Death Scythe with the help of his second Meister, Maka's mother.
